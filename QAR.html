WGL-QAR DATA DELETE GUIDE (AMM)

ACTION RESULT
On the WGL-QAR Front Panel: On the WGL-QAR Display:
Push the MODE button.
SYSTEM STATE comes into view.
Push the + button again and again until the STATUS message comes into view.
STATUS comes into view.
Push the SEL button.
ACQUISITION comes into view.
Push the + button again and again until the CAPACITY message comes into view.
CAPACITY comes into view.
Push the SEL button.
RAW PERCENTAGE comes into view.
Push the + button until the RECYCLE EOF AND WGL FILES message comes into view.
RECYCLE EOF AND WGL FILES comes into view.
Push the SEL button.
RECORDING LOST + TO CONTINUE comes into view.
NOTE: To prevent data-loss, you can push the MODE button at this time.
NOTE: If you do the next step, all recorder data will be erased.
Push the + button.
The WGL-QAR reboots. After reboot the WGL-QAR, is ready for operation.
